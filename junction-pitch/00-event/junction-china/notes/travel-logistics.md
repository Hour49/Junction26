# Travel logistics: Romanian student team to a hackathon in mainland China (late 2026 / 2027)

Research date: 2026-11-09. Scope: visa, flights, ground costs, tech/payments, funding, safety. About 15 tool calls; several official pages returned 404 (noted below).

## Visa: visa-free entry, L/M visas, 240-hour transit

### Takeaway
Romanian ordinary-passport holders can currently enter China visa-free for up to 30 days for business, tourism, family visits, exchange visits or transit, and a hackathon fits "business/exchange". The policy is confirmed only until 24:00 Beijing time on 31 Dec 2026. As of 31 Oct 2026 no extension into 2027 had been announced, so a 2027 trip may need an M or F visa from the Bucharest centre unless an extension comes (the 2026 extension was announced on 3 Nov 2025).

### Cited Findings
- The National Immigration Administration (NIA) list, compiled 17 Feb 2026, includes Romania under Europe. Ordinary-passport holders may enter visa-free for business, tourism, visits to relatives and friends, exchange visits and transit, for up to 30 days. Day counting starts at 00:00 the day after entry. The page states no end date. — [NIA country list](https://en.nia.gov.cn/n147418/n147463/c183390/content.html)
- Romania was added to the scheme from 30 Nov 2024, and its entry window is listed as 30 Nov 2024 to 31 Dec 2026. — [Windhorse Tour](https://windhorsetour.com/china-travel-tips/30-days-china-visa-free-countries-2025); [informat.ro](https://informat.ro/en/international/china-expands-visa-free-travel-program-for-48-countries-89882)
- The Romanian MFA (MAE) welcomed the waiver: 30 days for business, tourism, visits or transit, and travellers outside those purposes still need a visa. — [financialintelligence.ro citing MAE](https://financialintelligence.ro/mae-saluta-eliminarea-temporara-a-obligativitatii-vizei-pentru-romanii-care-calatoresc-in-china/)
- **Expiry:** the 30-day unilateral entry is "confirmed only until 24:00 on 31 December 2026 (Beijing time)", per an MFA notice of 3 Nov 2025. Whether it continues past that date was unknown as of 31 Oct 2026. — [Bliss Travel, verified 31 Oct 2026](https://blisstravel.life/blogs/travel-guide/china-visa-free-when-does-it-end); [travel-dealz](https://travel-dealz.com/?p=100410); [Time Out](https://www.timeout.com/news/europeans-can-now-visit-china-visa-free-until-the-end-of-2026-heres-what-that-means-for-your-next-trip-013026)
- The only 2027 extension found is country-specific. China extended visa-free entry for Russians to 31 Dec 2027, announced on 20 May 2026. No general extension was found. — [CGTN](https://news.cgtn.com/news/2026-05-20/China-extends-visa-free-policy-for-Russia-until-end-of-2027-1NiFHg4DlzG/share_amp.html); [Interfax](https://interfax.com/newsroom/top-stories/117662/)
- Sources conflict on in-country extension of a visa-free stay: Entrybrief says it is not possible; Globalcostdata suggests it may be. One aggregator (evisas.io) gives 14 days, which is outdated and conflicts with NIA. — [entrybrief](https://entrybrief.com/ro-passport/cn/); [globalcostdata](https://globalcostdata.com/visa/romania-to-china/); [evisas.io](https://evisas.io/visa-requirements/ro/cn)
- **240-hour (10-day) visa-free transit:** you must travel on to a third country or region with a confirmed onward ticket and use designated ports (reported as 65 ports in 24 provinces). It covers 55 nationalities, and Romania qualifies, but the 30-day scheme is more flexible while it lasts. Sources disagree on the count (55, 57 or 58). No expiry is written into the transit policy. — [chinafortravelers, verified 2026-05-04](https://chinafortravelers.com/tools/240-hour-transit/eligible/); [Shanghai gov 240-hour page (404 when fetched)](https://english.shanghai.gov.cn/en-VisaLatest/20260709/d70a1231ba4641ff9a024e53751ad480.html); [Intochinatrip 2027 guide](https://intochinatrip.com/china-visa-guide/)
- **Visa centre in Bucharest:** it takes only personal bank cards issued in EEA countries and offers only Regular and Express Level 2 service. RON fees were last re-set on 30 Sep 2023 at the BNR EUR/RON rate. The fee table page returned 404 and the amounts were not retrieved. Contact: bucharestcenter@visaforchina.org, +40 021 796 00 56. — [visaforchina BUH fee page](https://visaforchina.cn/BUH2_EN/generalinformation/visaknowledge/263487.shtml); [BUH about](https://www.visaforchina.cn/BUH2_EN/aboutus/263504.shtml)
- The VIP service is listed at "VIP 1: 207 RON" (unconfirmed context). — [BUH VIP notice](https://visaforchina.cn/BUH2_EN/generalinformation/news/282875.shtml)
- Embassy address: Sos. Nordului Nr. 2, Sector 1, 014101 Bucharest. — [travelchinaguide](https://www.travelchinaguide.com/embassy/romania.htm)

### Inferences
- For a trip in Nov–Dec 2026 the team needs no visa: just a passport, a return ticket, and arrival and departure before 31 Dec 2026. Carry the hackathon acceptance email or invitation anyway in case border officers ask about the "business/exchange" purpose.
- For 2027, watch MFA/NIA announcements in Nov–Dec 2026; last year's extension came on 3 Nov. If nothing is announced, apply for an M visa (business/commercial activity). An invitation letter from the organiser is the standard M-visa document, and F (exchange/visit) is an alternative. Book a visa-centre slot about 4–6 weeks ahead.
- The 240-hour transit only helps if the itinerary leaves China for a third place, for example Romania → Beijing → Hong Kong or Seoul. A round trip back to the origin country does not qualify.

## Flights from CLJ / OTP / BUD

### Takeaway
New since 4 Sep 2026: Air China flies Beijing–Bucharest–Zagreb three times a week (Mon/Wed/Fri), about 10 h nonstop from OTP to PEK, with a pre-launch fare estimate from about EUR 700. From Cluj, the cheapest routing is usually one stop via Istanbul on Turkish Airlines, roughly USD 270–950 one way depending on dates. Budapest also has China flights (not verified in this pass).

### Cited Findings
- Air China resumed scheduled Beijing–Bucharest flights on 4 Sep 2026, three times a week on Mon/Wed/Fri, with an A330-200 continuing to Zagreb after about a 3 h stop in Bucharest. It is the first direct Romania–China service since Tarom ended its route in 2003. — [Agerpres](https://agerpres.ro/english/2026/08/11/starting-september-4-romanians-will-be-able-to-fly-directly-to-china-three-times-a-week--1584068); [Beijing gov](https://english.beijing.gov.cn/latest/news/202609/t20260906_4852259.html); [cursdeguvernare.ro](https://cursdeguvernare.ro/zborurile-directe-bucuresti-beijing-sunt-reluate-dupa-23-de-ani-air-china-a-operat-vineri-prima-cursa-pe-aeroportul-henri-coanda.html)
- OTP→PEK takes about 9 h 50 min to 10 h and PEK→OTP about 11 h. Tickets were reported "from 700 euros" (pre-launch estimate). — [Romania Insider](https://www.romania-insider.com/bucharest-beijing-direct-flights-aug-2026); [informat.ro](https://informat.ro/en/current-affairs/direct-flights-bucharest-beijing-from-september-4-with-air-china-134325)
- On CLJ→Shanghai, Turkish Airlines via Istanbul is shown as the cheapest carrier, with total trip times of about 14–27 h. Sample fares: NZD 1,483–1,815 one way (June–July); Rome2rio estimates USD 270–950. — [Trip.com CLJ-SHA](https://www.trip.com/flights/cluj-napoca-to-shanghai/airfares-clj-sha); [Rome2rio](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Shanghai/Cluj-Napoca); [Turkish Airlines](https://www.turkishairlines.com/en/flights-from-shanghai-to-cluj-napoca)

### Inferences
- For a Beijing event, Cluj→Bucharest (domestic, or the train) plus the Air China direct flight is probably the simplest option. For Shanghai, Shenzhen or Hangzhou, one stop via Istanbul (Turkish), Doha (Qatar) or Dubai (Emirates) from CLJ or OTP, or the Budapest options, is likely. Budget about EUR 600–1,000 return per person, book 6–10 weeks ahead, and expect peaks around Chinese Golden Week (1–7 Oct) and Spring Festival (Feb 2027).

### Gaps

## Costs on the ground

### Takeaway
China is cheap for a student team. Backpacker days in Shanghai run about USD 25–50 (CNY 170–350), and metro rides are CNY 3–6. Hackathons often provide food and sometimes a venue to sleep in, so lodging for the non-event nights is the main cost.

### Cited Findings
- A Shanghai backpacker day with a hostel dorm costs about USD 25 (CNY 170); a comfortable day with a budget hotel and sit-down meals about USD 85 (CNY 575). — [travelofchina](https://www.travelofchina.com/how-much-does-a-day-in-shanghai-cost-2026/)
- A budget traveller spends USD 25–40/day; a hostel dorm saves USD 20–30/day versus a 3-star hotel. — [chinavigators](https://www.chinavigators.com/?p=3570)
- CNY 200–350/day covers a hostel bed, street food, transport and free sights. — [nomadotravel](https://www.nomadotravel.app/en/guides/shanghai-on-a-budget)
- Hostelz models about USD 72/day for Shanghai (a modelled figure, not observed prices). — [Hostelz](https://www.hostelz.com/backpacker-budget/cities/shanghai-china)
- Shanghai metro: CNY 3 for the first 6 km, then +1 per 10 km. Most central trips cost CNY 3–5, about CNY 5–15/day. — [travelofchina](https://www.travelofchina.com/how-much-does-a-day-in-shanghai-cost-2026/); [gotripzi](https://www.gotripzi.com/destinations/shanghai-cn/cost)
- Hackathon travel support examples: Junction 2026 (Espoo, 13–15 Nov 2026) offered EUR 300/100 travel grants to international participants, with a deadline of 6 Sep 2026. AdventureX 2025 (Hangzhou) reimbursed flights or trains for distant participants. — [adventure-x.org](https://adventure-x.org/en); search snippet on Junction 2026 grants (no primary page retrieved)

### Inferences
- A rough per-person budget for 7 days, excluding flights: lodging CNY 150–400/night (shared hotel room or hostel), food CNY 80–150/day, transport CNY 20–40/day, so about EUR 250–500. Add a China-bound flight of EUR 600–1,000.

### Gaps
- No verified 2026 hostel nightly rates; check Trip.com or Hostelworld. Shenzhen, Beijing and Hangzhou were not costed separately.

## Tech: internet, VPN, eSIM, payments, apps

### Takeaway
Google, GitHub (partly), OpenAI and Claude are not reliably reachable on Chinese networks. A roaming SIM or foreign eSIM routes traffic abroad and is the common, lower-risk workaround. VPN use by individuals is a legal grey area, rarely enforced against visitors, but it must be set up before arrival. Alipay and WeChat Pay now accept foreign Visa/Mastercard after passport verification; reported limits conflict.

### Cited Findings
- VPN status: guides describe only government-approved VPNs as technically legal, and enforcement against short-term visitors as rare but not zero. A Feb 2026 draft Cybercrime Law targets providers, not users (enactment not confirmed). VPN sites are blocked inside China, so install before arrival, and few VPNs work reliably. — [intotravelchina](https://intotravelchina.com/travel-guide/do-i-need-a-vpn-in-china.html); [comparitech](https://www.comparitech.com/blog/vpn-privacy/are-vpns-legal-in-china); [safelyo](https://safelyo.com/are-vpns-legal-in-china)
- International roaming or a foreign eSIM avoids the VPN question. Hotel, airport and venue Wi-Fi puts you back behind the firewall. — [intotravelchina](https://intotravelchina.com/travel-guide/do-i-need-a-vpn-in-china.html); [migaku](https://migaku.com/blog/language-fun/living-behind-the-great-firewall-vpn-and-internet-reality-for-expats-in-china)
- Payments: foreign Visa/Mastercard/JCB can be linked to Alipay and WeChat Pay after passport verification, without a Chinese bank account. A 3% fee applies on transactions over CNY 200 (per several guides). Foreign cards can't send P2P transfers or red packets. Home banks may block China charges, so notify them. — [mychina.guide](https://mychina.guide/questions/foreigners-use-alipay-wechat-pay-china-international-credit-cards); [realchinatrip](https://realchinatrip.com/blogs/payments-apps/how-to-use-alipay-and-wechat-pay-for-foreigners-setup-limits-comparison-table)
- Reported limits conflict: USD 5,000 per transaction and USD 50,000 per year after verification; CNY 6,000 per transaction and CNY 60,000 per year; and a May 2026 claim of CNY 35,000 per transaction, which another source disputes. Check the limit shown in the app. — [travelofchina](https://www.travelofchina.com/alipay-wechat-new-5000-limit-for-foreigners-2026/); [wanderinchina](https://www.wanderinchina.com/survival-guide/payment/alipay/)

### Inferences
- Before departure, each member should: install Alipay and WeChat and verify them with passport and card; buy a data eSIM (Airalo/Holafly-type, or a Romanian operator's roaming plan; check whether Orange/Vodafone/Digi include China); install Amap or Baidu Maps, DiDi (also inside Alipay), Trip.com, a translator (Pleco/Google Translate offline pack), and the 12306 rail app if needed. Clone repos and download models, packages and Docker images before flying.
- For hackathon dev work, expect GitHub to be slow or flaky and OpenAI/Anthropic APIs to be blocked on local networks. Use roaming data or a hotspot, and plan for Chinese model APIs (Qwen, DeepSeek, Kimi) if the hackathon requires them. Don't rely on venue Wi-Fi for blocked services.

### Gaps
- No official statement was found on GitHub, OpenAI or Anthropic reachability in 2026. Romanian operators' China roaming prices were not checked.

## Funding for Romanian/EU students

### Takeaway
I found no China-specific Junction travel grant for 2026; Junction China last ran in Chengdu in 2021. Funding will likely come from the organiser (some Chinese hackathons reimburse travel), company sponsors and UBB's faculty or student funds. Erasmus+ is a poor fit for a short hackathon in a non-programme country (unverified).

### Cited Findings

### Inferences
- Ask the organiser about travel grants first; Chinese government-backed and city-backed events (for example in Shenzhen or Hangzhou) often cover hotels or flights for foreign teams. Secondary sources: UBB faculty or student-association funds, Romanian or EU tech companies in Cluj, and the Chinese embassy or Confucius Institute at UBB for cultural-exchange framing.

## Safety and health

### Takeaway
China is generally low-crime for visitors. The main practical steps are travel medical insurance (an EHIC is not valid in China), routine vaccinations, carrying your passport (hotels register guests), and the visa-free date limits.

### Cited Findings
- MAE notes the visa waiver is purpose-limited (business, tourism, visits, transit), and other purposes require a visa. — [financialintelligence.ro citing MAE](https://financialintelligence.ro/mae-saluta-eliminarea-temporara-a-obligativitatii-vizei-pentru-romanii-care-calatoresc-in-china/)

### Inferences
- Buy travel medical insurance with at least EUR 30k cover plus repatriation (typical for students, unverified). Bring a passport valid at least 6 months beyond the trip. Register the trip with MAE's "Călătoresc în siguranță" app.

### Gaps
- No sourced MAE health or vaccination advice for China was retrieved; check mae.ro/travel-alerts and a travel clinic (routine vaccines plus Hep A/B are commonly advised).
