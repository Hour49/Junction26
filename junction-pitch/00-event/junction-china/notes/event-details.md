# Junction China hackathon: event details (researched 2026-11-09)

## Does Junction China exist, under what name, and which candidate is official?

### Takeaway
Yes. The official current edition is **JUNCTION CHINA 2026, "Hack What's Next"**, held in Beijing on 6 to 8 Nov 2026. It has **just finished**: today is 9 Nov 2026. In Chinese it ran as "Junction China 2026国际极客嘉年华" (International Geek Carnival), under the 2026 Zhongguancun Forum series. The official site is hackjunction.cn. Junction Oy (Finland) co-hosted it, so it is official. No "联结黑客松" naming was found. There was no 2025 China edition, and no next (2027) China edition has been announced.

### Cited Findings
- Official site title: "JUNCTION CHINA 2026: Hack What's Next". The site says "Junction China is officially launched by Junction" and that "JUNCTION joins three leading technology companies" (it does not name them). — [hackjunction.cn](https://hackjunction.cn)
- Full Chinese official name: "中关村论坛系列活动——中欧青年创新产业对接活动暨Junction China2026国际极客嘉年华" (Zhongguancun Forum series: China–Europe Youth Innovation Industry Matchmaking Event and Junction China 2026 International Geek Carnival). — [China News Service via Sina Finance, 2026-11-08](https://finance.sina.com.cn/roll/2026-11-08/doc-inixyrxh7498525.shtml)
- Media describe it as the first time the Junction brand has been held in China ("首次落地中国"). — [Sina Finance / CNS](https://finance.sina.com.cn/roll/2026-11-08/doc-inixyrxh7498525.shtml). This conflicts with the earlier history below, so treat "first" as PR framing. 2021 was billed as the first *flagship* event, and 2018 and 2021 events existed.
- Haidian District's official Weibo (海淀宣传) posted about "Junction China 2026国际极客嘉年华" on 2026-11-08: 40+ hours of nonstop development in Zhongguancun Science City, covering AI agents, embodied intelligence and smart wearables. — [Sina/Weibo post](https://www.sina.cn/weibo/detail/5363005702538422.html), [weibo.com](https://weibo.com/2/detail/5363005702538422)
- Community listing: "Junction China 2026 International Hackathon / 国际黑客松", 6 to 8 Nov 2026, free, links to hackjunction.cn. It was added on 2026-11-07. — [GitHub bovi/beijing-events #7](https://github.com/bovi/beijing-events/issues/7)

**Older China history (label: pre-2025)**
- 2018: Junction ran an event at Tsinghua University. Chinese and Korean universities sent top students to Junction Helsinki. — [Wikipedia: Junction (hackathon)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Junction_(hackathon))
- 2021: "Junction China 2021" was billed as "the first Junction flagship event in China": a 48-hour hackathon plus tech summit, planned for 3 to 5 Dec 2021 at Western International Expo City, Tianfu New Area, Chengdu. It was **postponed, new dates TBC**. FinnCham Beijing hosted the page, with support from the Finnish Business Council Beijing and Team Finland. Track partners were Nokia Shanghai Bell, Audi, Roblox and Urbstror. Contacts were finncham@finnchambj.org and topi.guo@hackjunction.com. — [FinnCham Beijing GlueUp event page](https://fbcbj.glueup.cn/event/34268)
- 2021: JunctionX Tianjin took place. HEBUT students won it. — [HEBUT news](https://finland.hebut.edu.cn/News/1c222af5384849b3980547c9d6878939.htm) (title only seen in search; page not fetched)

### Inferences
- The candidates and their status:
  - Junction China 2026 (Beijing) is the official current edition.
  - Junction China 2021 (Chengdu) was postponed, and I found no evidence it ever took place.
  - JunctionX Tianjin 2021 was a local JunctionX spin-off.
  - The 2018 Tsinghua event predates both.
- I found no Shanghai or Shenzhen Junction or JunctionX events.

### Gaps
- I found no evidence of a 2025 Junction China edition.
- No Junction China 2027 has been announced. Its city and dates are unknown.
- The global hackjunction.com site was not checked for a Junction China listing.

## Dates, city, venue, format, size, language

### Takeaway
The event ran 6 to 8 Nov 2026 at the ZGC International Technology Trade Center (北京中关村国际技术交易中心) in Haidian, Beijing. It was in person, "semi-closed", with 40+ hours of hacking, and drew nearly 300 participants.

### Cited Findings
- Schedule (UTC+8):
  - 6 Nov: 13:00 check-in, 14:00 opening (international forum), 19:00 hacking starts.
  - Hacking ends 8 Nov at 17:30.
  - The site calls it "A 40+ hour semi-closed international hackathon" and does not define "semi-closed".
  - — [hackjunction.cn](https://hackjunction.cn)
- Venue: ZGC International Technology Trade Center, Beijing. — [hackjunction.cn](https://hackjunction.cn); [GitHub listing](https://github.com/bovi/beijing-events/issues/7)
- The event was co-organized with Shangdi Subdistrict, Haidian District (海淀区上地街道). — [Sina Finance / CNS](https://finance.sina.com.cn/roll/2026-11-08/doc-inixyrxh7498525.shtml)
- Participants: "近300名" (nearly 300) contestants.
  - They came from 20+ universities in Beijing and across China, including Tsinghua, Peking University and BUPT.
  - They also included "OPC" young entrepreneurs and open-source contest enthusiasts.
  - There were **7 international teams**.
  - — [Sina Finance / CNS](https://finance.sina.com.cn/roll/2026-11-08/doc-inixyrxh7498525.shtml)
- The site is bilingual (Chinese and English). The 60K+ participants and 110+ nationalities figures on it are global Junction stats, not figures for this event. — [hackjunction.cn](https://hackjunction.cn)
- The program included an industry matchmaking session where **11 Finnish startups and investor representatives** pitched in AI, medtech and new materials. — [Sina Finance / CNS](https://finance.sina.com.cn/roll/2026-11-08/doc-inixyrxh7498525.shtml)

### Inferences
- The working language was likely Chinese with English support, given the bilingual site and the 7 international teams. This is not stated anywhere.

### Gaps
- The official working language was not stated.
- No exact venue street address was given.

## Organizers and partners

### Takeaway
Government-backed. Beijing S&T bodies guided the event, and Junction Oy co-hosted it with the Beijing International S&T Cooperation Center, 氪星创服 (KR STAR Innovation) and Shangdi Subdistrict.

### Cited Findings
- Guiding units: Beijing Municipal Science and Technology Commission (北京市科学技术委员会) and the Zhongguancun Science Park Administrative Committee (中关村科技园区管理委员会). — [Sina Finance / CNS](https://finance.sina.com.cn/roll/2026-11-08/doc-inixyrxh7498525.shtml)
- Organizers: Beijing International Science and Technology Cooperation Center (北京国际科技合作中心), **Junction Oy (Finland)**, 氪星创服 (KR STAR Innovation) and Haidian Shangdi Subdistrict. — [Sina Finance / CNS](https://finance.sina.com.cn/roll/2026-11-08/doc-inixyrxh7498525.shtml)
- At the event, these bodies and the Finnish Aalto innovation association (芬兰阿尔托创新协会, probably Aaltoes) launched a "Beijing–Finland frontier industry cooperation mechanism". It covers talent, industry matchmaking and two-way overseas expansion. — [Sina Finance / CNS](https://finance.sina.com.cn/roll/2026-11-08/doc-inixyrxh7498525.shtml)
- Partners listed on the site:
  - Challenge and track partners: Xiaomi MiMo, LLVISION, GALBOT.
  - Other partners: Beijing International Science and Technology Cooperation Center, OSCHINA, BerryCode, KR STAR Innovation, ZION, Unity Developer Community, Tuanjie Codely, FinnCham China, Cornea Sense, SILKWAY, TNIH.
  - Ecosystem partners: SLUSH, DASH, maria 01, Aaltoes, Startup Sauna.
  - — [hackjunction.cn](https://hackjunction.cn)
- Supporting accounts tagged on Weibo: @北京发布, @北京日报, @科技北京, @中关村科学城. — [Weibo post](https://www.sina.cn/weibo/detail/5363005702538422.html)

### Gaps
- The "three leading technology companies" that run the event with Junction are not named. They are probably the three challenge setters, but this is unconfirmed.

## Challenges and tracks (2026)

### Takeaway
There were three tracks, each set by a Beijing frontier-tech company.

### Cited Findings
- **Artificial Intelligence**, set by **Xiaomi MiMo**: LLMs, multimodal AI, AI agents, multi-agent collaboration. — [hackjunction.cn](https://hackjunction.cn)
- **Embodied AI & Robotics**, set by **GALBOT (银河通用)**: humanoid robots, robot learning, dexterous manipulation. — [hackjunction.cn](https://hackjunction.cn)
- **Smart Wearables**, set by **LLVISION (亮亮视野)**: AR/VR/MR, 3D vision, digital twins, spatial intelligence. — [hackjunction.cn](https://hackjunction.cn)
- Press confirms the three companies set challenges tied to their R&D. Press names the themes as AI and agents, robotics and embodied intelligence, and smart glasses and spatial computing. — [Sina Finance / CNS](https://finance.sina.com.cn/roll/2026-11-08/doc-inixyrxh7498525.shtml)

### Gaps
- The detailed challenge briefs and judging criteria were not published on the landing page.
- No winners or results were found as of 2026-11-09.

## Prizes, travel, accommodation, food

### Takeaway
The total prize pool was **RMB 90,000**: RMB 30,000 per track, split 20k / 6k / 4k. Track winners get direct qualification for Junction 2027 in Helsinki. Meals and travel support were provided for eligible participants from outside Beijing, Tianjin and Hebei.

### Cited Findings
- Prizes:
  - Total pool: RMB 90,000.
  - Per track: 1st RMB 20,000, 2nd RMB 6,000, 3rd RMB 4,000.
  - Track winners "qualify directly for Junction 2027 in Helsinki".
  - Career and incubation opportunities at partner companies.
  - Finishers get a certificate plus event and partner merchandise.
  - — [hackjunction.cn](https://hackjunction.cn)
- Press says winners also get joint R&D with the challenge companies and direct entry to Junction's global finals in Finland next year. — [Sina Finance / CNS](https://finance.sina.com.cn/roll/2026-11-08/doc-inixyrxh7498525.shtml)
- What participants get: "Free registration, daily essentials, meals and travel support for eligible participants outside Beijing, Tianjin and Hebei." No amounts are given. — [hackjunction.cn](https://hackjunction.cn)

### Gaps
- Accommodation is not mentioned.
- The amount and rules for travel support are not stated.
- It is unclear whether the Helsinki qualification includes flights.

## Application process

### Takeaway
Registration was free and done individually through an external Chinese form (jsjform). Participants could come solo or in teams of up to 5. No deadline, selection criteria or acceptance rate was published.

### Cited Findings
- Registration form: https://izmunair.jsjform.com/f/ofB2VN (not on a Junction domain). — [hackjunction.cn](https://hackjunction.cn)
- Team size: solo or up to 5 people. Each member registers individually, then reports the team before the event or forms one on site. — [hackjunction.cn](https://hackjunction.cn)
- Who it targets: developers, engineers, designers, PMs, makers, students and non-technical creatives. No formal age or nationality rules are stated. — [hackjunction.cn](https://hackjunction.cn)
- Fee: free. — [hackjunction.cn](https://hackjunction.cn); [GitHub listing](https://github.com/bovi/beijing-events/issues/7)
- One aggregator reportedly gave the registration deadline as 6 Nov 2026. This came only from a search-engine summary and is unverified.

### Inferences
- "Semi-closed" plus the free travel support suggests some screening of applicants. This is unconfirmed.

### Gaps
- No deadline, selection criteria or acceptance rate was found.
- Age and international eligibility rules were not found.
