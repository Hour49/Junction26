# Junction China: history of Junction-branded hackathons in China and their place in the Junction network

Research date: 2026-11-09. Every fact below carries its date and source. Anything I could not source is under "Gaps". Several primary pages could not be fetched: the Junction Medium post returned 403, hackjunction.com/about returned only a title, and the JunctionX presentation PDF could not be parsed.

## 1. When did Junction first come to China, and which editions have happened?

### Takeaway
Junction's first China activity was "JUNCTION x Tsinghua", held at Tsinghua University in Beijing on 18-20 May 2018. JunctionX-branded events followed in Chengdu (Aug 2020) and Tianjin (May 2021). The first "Junction China" flagship was planned for Chengdu in Dec 2021 but was postponed. A Junction China edition did run in Chengdu in 2023 (finished around late Sep or early Oct 2023). The current edition, Junction China 2026, ran 6-8 Nov 2026 in Beijing (Zhongguancun). I found no Junction or JunctionX event in Hong Kong, Shanghai or Shenzhen.

### Cited Findings
**Timeline of China editions**

**2018-05-18 to 20, "JUNCTION x Tsinghua", Beijing**
- Tsinghua and Junction co-hosted it as part of Tsinghua's 7th creative competition, and teams from Aalto University were invited. — [Tsinghua News, 2018-11-26](https://www.tsinghua.edu.cn/info/1175/19247.htm); [Tsinghua News (headline version)](https://www.tsinghua.edu.cn/info/1173/17718.htm)
- Wikipedia: in 2018 Junction "held an event at Tsinghua University in China", working with Chinese and South Korean universities to bring high-performing students to the Helsinki event. — [Wikipedia: Junction (hackathon)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Junction_(hackathon))
- A 2022 Korea Times article lists Beijing among the JunctionX host cities, alongside Tokyo, Hanoi, Singapore, Budapest, Exeter and Barcelona. This is probably the 2018 Tsinghua event, but the article does not name it. — [Korea Times, May 2022](https://www.koreatimes.co.kr/www/tech/2022/05/133_328528.html)
- A Finland Abroad (Embassy of Finland, Vietnam) page from 2018 says JunctionX "has been to Japan, China, Budapest, Jeddah". — [finlandabroad.fi, JunctionX Hanoi 2018](https://finlandabroad.fi/web/vnm/current-affairs/-/asset_publisher/h5w4iTUJhNne/content/junctionxhanoi-2018/384951) (seen as a search snippet only)

**2020-08-28 to 30, "JUNCTION X Chengdu 2020"**

- Programme: track challenges announced 28 Aug, a track-partner workshop on 29 Aug, and track and overall winner awards on 30 Aug.

**2021-05 (ended 23 May 2021), "JunctionX Tianjin"**

- Host: Junction China. Listed by FinnCham China / Finnish Business Council Beijing, with special support from FBCB and Team Finland.
- Track partners: Nokia Shanghai Bell, Audi, Roblox and Urbstror.
- Targets: 300+ on-site hackers "from leading universities across China", 300+ industry attendees, 8 keynote speakers, 10,000+ live-stream viewers, 50+ media partners and 1M+ media reach.

- The listing is titled "Postponed – New dates TBC" and gives no reason.

**2023 (concluded by about 2 Oct 2023), "Junction China 2023", Chengdu**
- Junction's LinkedIn post: "the just-finished Junction China 2023 was indeed a sparking bomb into the Chengdu, China startup ecosystem. It created a unique platform for university students all around China to collaborate face-to-face with some of the leading tech giants."
- Hashtags: #JunctionChina and #Junction2023. The linked Medium article is by 郭海峰 (Topi).
- The LinkedIn activity ID decodes to 2023-10-02 16:05 UTC (my own decoding of the ID timestamp).
- — [Junction LinkedIn post](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/hackjunction_how-junction-impacts-the-innovative-atmosphere-activity-7114641503045451778-QRZu); [Medium article (403 on fetch; search snippet only)](https://hackjunction.medium.com/how-junction-impacts-the-innovative-atmosphere-of-the-young-tech-talents-in-china-8d5b61e314a4)

**2026-11-06 to 08, "Junction China 2026 – Hack What's Next", Beijing**
- Venue: ZGC International Technology Trade Center (北京中关村国际技术交易中心).
- Schedule: opening forum on 6 Nov at 14:00, then a 40+ hour hackathon from 6 Nov 19:00 to 8 Nov 17:30.
- Free entry. Teams of 1-5, and teams can form on site.

**No Hong Kong, Shanghai or Shenzhen editions found**
- Searches for "JunctionX Shanghai/Shenzhen/Hong Kong" turned up no such events. — [search, 2026-11-09]. This is negative evidence, not proof.

### Inferences
- The person behind Junction China appears to be the same since at least 2021: Topi Guo / 郭海峰, with a hackjunction.com email address. He is the contact for 2021 and the author of the 2023 article.
- The 2020 Chengdu JunctionX and the 2021/2023 Chengdu editions share a venue and the Tianfu New Area link. This points to backing from the Chengdu or Tianfu local government in that period. The move to Beijing ZGC in 2026 looks like a new local host; the Beijing International S&T Cooperation Center is listed as a partner.
- "First Junction flagship in China" (2021) means "Junction China" as distinct from the community-run JunctionX events before it.

### Gaps
- Participant numbers for 2018 Tsinghua, 2020 Chengdu, 2021 Tianjin, 2023 Chengdu and 2026 Beijing. Only the 2021 targets (300+ hackers) are published.

- Whether anything ran in 2019, 2022, 2024 or 2025. I found none.
- Exact dates of Junction China 2023.
- The organizer of JunctionX Tianjin 2021 and of JunctionX Chengdu 2020.

## 3. How does Junction license or organize satellite events, and who runs Junction China?

### Takeaway
JunctionX is a community-licence or franchise model: local teams (often student groups) run their own hackathon under the Junction brand, with support from Junction. Regional "flagship" events (Junction Asia, Junction China) are run with the core team or a close partner. Junction Asia is run with Korean non-profit Shift and the City of Busan. Junction China is described as "officially launched by Junction". Its lead is a Junction-email staffer (Topi Guo), backed by local government and FinnCham partners. That makes it closer to a core-run regional flagship than to a volunteer-run JunctionX.

### Cited Findings
**How JunctionX works**
- Wikipedia: "JunctionX… launched in 2018, is a global program through which local teams can organize a hackathon event in their own home city." Cities include Tokyo, Hanoi, Budapest, Seoul and Thuwal, with Exeter, Algiers and Barcelona added in 2020. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Junction_(hackathon))
- Search summary of Junction material: the JunctionX program "was created for local tech communities around the world to have a way to create their own hackathon", and all JunctionX events are created independently by local communities with Junction's support. — search snippets (secondary; primary JunctionX page not fetched)
- The first Japanese JUNCTION X was hosted by Kyutech, 26-28 Sep 2025. It was student-run with about 80 participants and called "the local spin-off of JUNCTION". — [Kyutech news](https://www.kyutech.ac.jp/english/en-news/topics/entry-11704.html) (search snippet)

**Junction Asia**
- Run by Shift, a Seoul student non-profit (president Lee Hyun-sae), with the Busan city government and Busan Tourism Organization; Busan and Junction signed an MOU on 2022-05-03.
- Junction CEO at the time: Akseli Aho.
- JunctionX Seoul ran in 2019 (offline) and in 2020 and 2021 (online).
- — [Korea Times, May 2022](https://www.koreatimes.co.kr/www/tech/2022/05/133_328528.html)

**Who runs Junction China**
- hackjunction.cn: Junction China is "officially launched by Junction, one of Europe's most influential hackathons", runs with three challenge partners and links to Junction's global site. — [hackjunction.cn](https://hackjunction.cn)
- In 2021 the host is named as "Junction China", with Topi Guo at a @hackjunction.com address and support from FBCB and Team Finland. — [FBCB](https://fbcbj.glueup.cn/event/junction-china-2021-postponed-new-dates-tbc-34268)

**Junction (core) background**
- Founded in 2015 in Helsinki as an Aaltoes-linked student non-profit. Headquartered in Espoo.
- Junction 2025: 14-16 Nov 2025 at Hype Hybridiarena, Espoo; 1,500+ participants from 111 nationalities; 291 projects.
- — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Junction_(hackathon))

### Inferences
- The legal entity behind Junction China (a Chinese company, a representative office, or partner-run under licence) is not public. The "Junction China" naming, the hackjunction.com staff email and the Helsinki qualification all suggest closer control than a JunctionX licence.

### Gaps
- Licence terms or fees for JunctionX: the primary PDF ([UPT "Junction X Presentation", May 2026](https://upt.edu.al/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Junction-X-Presentation.pdf)) could not be parsed.
- The legal entity operating hackjunction.cn.

## 4. Does winning Junction China lead to Junction Helsinki? What is Junction 2026?

### Takeaway
Yes, for 2026. Winners of each Junction China 2026 track "qualify directly" for the Junction 2027 flagship in Finland. The site does not say whether travel is paid. Junction Asia has a similar ticket. Junction 2026 itself runs 13-15 Nov 2026 at Hype Arena, Kivenlahti, Espoo (Helsinki metro area), with about 2,000 participants and about €100k in prizes advertised.

### Cited Findings
**Junction China to Helsinki**
- hackjunction.cn: track winners "qualify directly" for the Junction 2027 flagship in Helsinki. — [hackjunction.cn](https://hackjunction.cn)
- Earlier precedent: in 2018 Junction worked with Chinese and Korean universities to bring high-performing students to Helsinki. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Junction_(hackathon))

**Junction Asia to Helsinki (comparison)**
- The final winner of Junction Asia 2023 (Busan, 18-20 Aug 2023) was eligible for Junction 2023 in Finland.
- Junction Asia 2023 numbers: about 300 participants selected from 975 applicants in 27 countries; about 15M won in prizes.
- Team 고진감래 won Junction Asia 2022, then won Junction 2022 overall in Finland with "하루", an AI app that draws a picture from a diary entry.
- — [NewsSeoul](https://newsseoul.co.kr/news/view/1065568819434158)

**Junction 2026 (main event)**
- 13-15 Nov 2026 in the Helsinki metropolitan area, with about 2,000 builders over 48 hours. — [Espoo city events](https://www.espoo.fi/en/events/espooevents:agp767vqb4) (search snippet); [hackjunction.app pre-registration](https://hackjunction.app/hackathons/junction-2026-pre-registration)
- €100k in prizes and 2,000 participants. — [HackerNoon "12 hackathons worth your fall and winter"](https://hackernoon.com/12-hackathons-worth-your-fall-and-winter) (search snippet)
- Venue Hype Arena, Kivenlahti (Espoo); registration deadline 6 Sep 2026; teams of 1-4. Sourced from a LinkedIn post, reported only in a search summary.

**Junction's network-wide stats (self-reported)**
- 60K+ participants, 8,000+ projects, 110+ nationalities, 500+ universities and 200+ partners. — [hackjunction.cn](https://hackjunction.cn)

### Inferences
- Junction China 2026 is a week before Junction 2026. The qualification therefore points to Junction 2027, as the site says, not to this year's event.

### Gaps
- Whether flights or accommodation to Finland are covered for Junction China winners.
- Whether 2021 or 2023 Junction China winners went to Helsinki.

## 5. Participant reviews and experiences

### Takeaway
I found almost no first-person participant accounts of the China editions in English or Chinese, whether on LinkedIn, Medium, Zhihu or Xiaohongshu. Evidence on how international the events were is indirect. The China events drew participants mainly from Chinese universities, with a Finnish or Aalto link (Aalto teams in 2018; HEBUT's Finland campus in 2021). The 2026 site is bilingual and calls itself "international".

### Cited Findings
- 2018 Tsinghua event: teams from Aalto University were invited. — [Tsinghua News](https://www.tsinghua.edu.cn/info/1175/19247.htm)
- 2021 Chengdu: hackers "from leading universities across China". — [FBCB](https://fbcbj.glueup.cn/event/junction-china-2021-postponed-new-dates-tbc-34268)
- 2023 Chengdu: "university students all around China" collaborating with tech giants. — [Junction LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/hackjunction_how-junction-impacts-the-innovative-atmosphere-activity-7114641503045451778-QRZu)
- 2026 Beijing: billed as "Junction China 2026 International Hackathon / 国际黑客松" on an expat-oriented Beijing events list. — [bovi/beijing-events #7](https://github.com/bovi/beijing-events/issues/7)

### Inferences
- The FinnCham listing and the expat events listing suggest the events reach Beijing's foreign community. The partner mix and RMB prizes suggest mostly domestic participants.

### Gaps
