# Plain words: the grandma test

The test: read each spoken sentence and ask "would my grandma, who has never used ChatGPT, get this the first time she hears it, in a loud room?" If not, rewrite it.

## Rules
- One idea per sentence. About 12 words or fewer. Say "we", "you", "your".
- Short, common words. A word with 3+ syllables needs a reason to stay.
- Things people can see beat abstract ideas: "the box of milk is missing", not "inventory discrepancy".
- Numbers: one per sentence, rounded, with a unit people feel ("1 in 20 orders", "10 minutes a day"), never "4.87%".
- Name the person, not the segment: "the picker in the warehouse", not "logistics operators".
- Product and partner names are fine; explain them once ("Valio Aimo, the big food wholesaler").
- If a technical word must stay (e.g. a peer asks "how?"), say the plain version first, then the word: "it learns from old orders (machine learning)".
