## Rules this gives the deck
1. First 12 seconds: a person, a moment, or one number. Not "Hi, we are team…".
2. A named persona and a callback at the close (λpump).
3. One sourced number on the problem slide (Walkthrough).
4. A measured result against a baseline on the "why it holds" slide (DroneStalker).
5. Every finalist had a live URL or a QR code: put one on the close slide and leave it up for Q&A.
6. Videos silent with burned-in captions; she narrates. Audio failed for 2 of 5.
7. Label footage "Built this weekend" or "Vision".
8. One speaker. Keep "who did what" and "built this weekend vs before" as backup slides.
