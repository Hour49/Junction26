# Junction hackathon videos: catalogue for pitch prep

Built 2026-11-10 for team Hour49. Context: [junction-info.md](../00-event/junction-info.md) (sections 2C and 2D).

**How this was built.** The official channel is **Junction, `@JunctionHackathon`** ([channel](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCZxmJDpKoOCzHXtkdMKLcug)); `@hackjunction` does not exist on YouTube. I used yt-dlp to list its Videos, Live and Shorts tabs (136 items) and ran about 50 YouTube searches (`ytsearch`) by year, partner and project name. Web searches for Devpost or LinkedIn video links found nothing new. The WAVELET README links its demo video; the BREAKDANCE README links none.

**Verification.** All 88 URLs below were checked on 2026-11-10 with `https://www.youtube.com/oembed?url=…`. 86 returned 200. Two returned 401 (embedding disabled) but yt-dlp read their metadata, so they exist; they are marked *(no-embed)*. Durations and upload dates come from yt-dlp.

**Labels.** *Main* = the Helsinki/Espoo main event. *JunctionX* = a satellite event. *Junction side event* = another Junction-run hackathon, such as Defence or Supercell AI. Placements come from the official video descriptions or `00-event/junction-info.md`; anything weaker is marked **UNVERIFIED**.

**Submission videos are 2 minutes long.** Every 2025, 2024, 2023 and 2022 team demo found runs about 2:00. They show what the submission format actually looks like.

## Gaps

- **2024 finals are missing.** The official channel has no 2024 closing ceremony and no 2024 top-5 pitches, only the opening ceremony, the vlog, partner interviews and the aftermovie. No video was found for **BREAKDANCE** (1st overall 2024): its README links none, and searches by name and author found nothing. The "AI-generated rap video" from that pitch was not found either.
- **2025 closing ceremony.** No full stream is on the channel, only the five edited pitch cuts.
- **No 2025 submission videos found** for DroneStalker, Walkthrough or λpump, or for the Aalto track winner ApprenticeCircle+.
- **2023 winner.** "Don't blink" may be "Driving Change – In the Blink for Safety"; this is unconfirmed.
- **Track placements** for most team demos are unknown, since uploaders rarely state them.
- **No official Junction pitching-tips video exists** on the channel. The closest substitutes are the EIT talk, the winners' interview and partner interviews.
- **Limits of the search.** Discovery used YouTube search via yt-dlp. Devpost, LinkedIn and the Junction platform galleries were not crawled, and LinkedIn-hosted videos are not covered.
