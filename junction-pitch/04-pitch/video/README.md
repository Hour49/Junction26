# 04-pitch/video

The submission video, aim for 1:50–2:00 (every 2025 finalist video found is about 2:00; the 2026 limit is unconfirmed, check the platform on Friday), on YouTube or Vimeo, public or unlisted (pitching.md §1e, tooling in §5.2). Keep the script and shot list as `script.md` and the final link in `link.md`. Do not commit large video files; store them outside the repo and note where. For examples, see the 2025 winners' 2-minute videos in [../../02-reference/junction-videos.md](../../02-reference/junction-videos.md).
