## 1. Answer

**1. Nobody has measured the thing we need.** No paper ranks a confidence signal against exact per-image count correctness from a detector, on floor plans or anywhere else. The three reports ran more than 40 queries between them (web search, arXiv, OpenReview, CVF listings for CVPR 2025/2026, ICCV 2025, WACV 2025/2026, Crossref, Semantic Scholar citation lists, GitHub sweeps) and found box-level conformal detection, one-sided count losses and counting papers that report MAE, nothing on exact-match count correctness (reports a, b, c, "Looked for and did not find"). The nearest measured numbers, all different targets:
- per-image Pearson 0.59-0.66 between ContrastiveConf and per-image AP on COCO (0.28-0.44 under shift), while the mean confidence of the top 100 predictions correlates at -0.58 to -0.62 (2412.01782, Table 2; **read**, re-checked 01:24);
- Pearson r = 0.68 and 0.73 between a fish-sonar counter's predicted uncertainty and its absolute count error, 500 training images (Tarling et al., PLOS ONE 2022; **read** by a);
- a regressor for per-image counting quality (Jaccard) at R² 0.7765, of which 0.6740 comes from the detector's own count and mean box confidence plus sharpness (2506.22438, Table 4; **read** by a, abstract re-checked);
- a supervised "the detector missed a person" classifier at Safety AUROC 92.9 on COCO val and 80.3 on shifted COCO-O, against 52-54 for unsupervised OOD scores (2603.25499, Table 2; **read** by a and c; recall among accepted images 64.3% to 84.5% at 5% FPR is in the abstract, re-checked);
- fused per-field correctness scores for document extraction: AUROC 0.90-0.99 against 0.54-0.74 for a VLM's own confidence (2609.20110; abstract re-checked), 0.928 against 0.705 for log-probabilities on 267 test samples (2606.24420; abstract re-checked), 0.916 for agreement of four distinct readers on chemical structure images (2608.22183; abstract re-checked).
None is a count. The honest statement is that the choice of score is open and must be settled by a measurement on our dev split (Experiments, section 8).

**2. Cheapest signals, ordered by evidence and by cost.**
- *Plain confidence of the kept detections, aggregated carefully.* Per symbol, plain max-softmax already ranks errors on 44 real floor plans: accuracy at a 50% review budget 88.9 vs 86.6 for random, BALD 89.2, oracle 92.1, variation ratio 86.3 (Jakubik et al., AAAI-22, Table 3; **read**, re-checked). Per image, averaging all confidences misranks: mean confidence of the top 100 predictions is negative (above), and summing or averaging detection uncertainty gives AUROC 18.8-27.1 on three of six detector/dataset cells for rejecting out-of-distribution images, while the mean of the 2-3 most confident detections gives 94.1-97.7 (2307.00934, Table 2; **read** by a and c). Aggregation is the lever. A benchmark of confidence scores for classifiers found nothing that beats plain maximum softmax across failure sources (2211.15259; **read** by a), so the plain score is the baseline every fancier score must beat on dev.
- *Poisson-binomial count distribution from calibrated per-detection scores* (2204.05276): P(modal count) and the count entropy take a few lines. The authors report count accuracy 86.5% for the post-hoc version against 76.6-82.9% for parametric heads (Table C.2; **read**, re-checked) and show a calibration curve; I found no ranking number, and the counts are 0 to 3+, not about 9.
- *Agreement of architecturally distinct readers plus a cheap validity filter.* Four distinct recognizers: agreement AUROC 0.916 (0.880-0.952); three of four accepted 52.1% of images at 98.5% precision, two of four 81.7% at 88.8%; re-rendering the prediction and comparing pixels is at chance (0.547) (2608.22183; abstract re-checked). Two different OCR programs plus a dictionary: 99.74% of the words not flagged were right (reCAPTCHA, Science 2008; **read** by c). Agreement of *samples of one model* is weak: Spearman 0.20-0.59, and 48% of the high-agreement answers of the most consistent model were wrong (2607.08065; **read** by a and c). It works only when the readers fail differently.
- *A small learned classifier on [detector outputs, image quality, symbol density, spread across views, rule flags]* trained on dev outcomes: the pattern behind 2609.20110, 2606.24420, 2603.25499, 2506.22438 and the XGBoost per-image recall/F1 predictor of 2110.02459 (**read** by a). In ExtractConf the ablation puts OCR/quality features alone (AUC 0.896) above log-probabilities (0.880), and the second, differently built reading adds the largest single step (+1.7 AUC points) (**read** by c). It needs labelled right/wrong outcomes, so it is a Saturday item, not a tonight item.
- *Count spread across views of one detector* (four rotations, two tile offsets): the only test of count stability as an error signal is negative (|rho| < 0.1 on KITTI, while a box-agreement score reached rho 0.81; 2509.12871 §IV-G; **read** by a). Views of one model share its blind spots (simulation (b): two readings with shared difficulty underestimate the misses 5-6 times). Cheap to compute; measure before trusting.
- *Rules across answers* (windows+doors against rooms, counts against the schedule, printed against measured area): high precision, low recall. Model Assertions flagged items were real errors in 88-100% of 50 samples per rule, yet caught only 4 of 32 (12.5%) of human-label errors (2003.01668; **read** by b); a rule-based financial verifier had precision 100% and recall 52.8% falling to 3.7% as the tolerance loosened (2605.29586; **read** by b). Our own stage 8 found 0 of the 14 real conflicts and raised 0 false alarms on the 102 agree entries (docs/PIPELINE.md:74): a flag can be trusted when it fires, and says nothing when it is silent.

**3. How to state the guarantee honestly.** Pick the unit, the score, alpha and delta; calibrate on labelled plans like the ones served; report an exact upper bound, not the observed error. Sentence to use:

> On N plans checked by hand, drawn from the sources we serve, the plans our gate marks sure had every answer right in at least 90% of cases, with 90% confidence over the choice of those N plans (exact binomial bound, threshold chosen on a separate split). The gate marks sure X% of plans. This is an average over sheet types; per source (CubiCasa-like, scans, phone photos, rotated) the numbers are: ...

What each clause costs, from the sources:
- *The unit is the plan.* Answers of one plan share the image. Clustering halves the effective sample: design effect 1+(m-1)ρ, with ρ the within-cluster correlation of the threshold indicator, not of the scores (2608.21262; 1,000 rows drawn as documents carry 217 independent observations at the median and 485 at p=0.90; **read** by a and c). On receipts the document-level certificate covered 0.060 against 0.171 when fields were treated as independent (2608.14639; abstract re-checked: "doc-iid 0.060 ... honestly near-vacuous today").
- *The bound.* Selection with Guaranteed Risk (1705.08500) and Learn-then-Test (2110.01052) turn any score plus labelled calibration data into "wrong at most alpha among accepted, with probability 1-delta over the calibration draw". Exact-tail arithmetic, recomputed by me (delta 0.10, one fixed threshold): the smallest number of accepted calibration plans with k wrong is 22, 38, 52, 65 for k=0,1,2,3 at alpha 0.10 and 45, 77, 105, 132 at alpha 0.05; with 100 thresholds tried (Bonferroni) 66 accepted plans at alpha 0.10 and zero errors. With the Hoeffding-Bentkus factor e (as printed in the LTT paper) report a gets 47, 65, 80 for k=1,2,3 at alpha 0.10. These are floors with a perfect gate; realistic needs are higher: about 180 accepted units for an 80% chance of certifying a 0.90 precision target when the true error is 5%, and 13,407 per unit at 9% true error against 10% (2609.22048, section 4; **read** by c); 800 expert checks certified nothing at a thin margin (2611.01566, Table 4; **read** by c); with 134 calibration units only about 18.5% of cases certify at alpha 0.10 (2603.08907; **read** by c).
- *Selecting and testing the threshold on the same sample inflates the claim.* Choose it on one split, certify on another: that doubles the need (299 + 368 units at alpha 0.01; 2610.35092; **read** by c). Thresholds picked on one audit split stayed hidden on a fresh one in 28 of 30 cases (2610.29067; abstract **read**).
- *Report how often the gate opens.* A valid procedure that rarely fires can be wrong in up to 69% of the draws in which it fires, since the bound is delta/P_fire (2610.09541, Proposition 3; **read** by b).
- *It is an average, not a promise per plan.* Conformal risk control states "no guarantee for each test point" (2208.02814); a method that hits 0.90 average coverage still had a per-image standard deviation of 0.25 (2504.07611, Table 1; **read** by a).
- *Shift breaks it.* Naive empirical thresholds broke the stated budget in 49-73% of synthetic trials; certified rules overran in 9-30% of grouped-shift trials (2606.15153; **read** by b and c, numbers re-checked in the saved text by c); a pooled gate protected the average institution and failed at 40% of the individual ones (2606.20115; abstract **read**); static control violated the target on 14 of 16 cross-dataset transfers (2606.29054; **read** by b). Calibrate per source or name the source in the claim.
- *Label noise and reviewers sit under the number.* Re-annotating detection benchmarks added up to +60% (KITTI) and +40% (COCO) objects, mostly small, occluded or dense ones (2609.21822; abstract **read**); 5% flipped calibration labels cut the share released from 85% to 57% at the same stated guarantee (2607.24562 Table 9; **read** by c); a median expert accepted 17% of wrong suggestions and confidence flags did not change that (2103.04725 §6.2; **read**, re-checked).

What not to say: "90% of sure answers are right" without N, the source mix and the bound; "the model is 95% confident" for an uncalibrated score; any per-plan statement from an average guarantee; "valid at 95%" without the share of plans released.

**4. A ceiling on how many plans can be marked sure.** If the base wrong-answer rate is mu and the target alpha, any distribution-free method must leave at least (mu-alpha)/(1-alpha) of the answers unmarked (2606.29054, Proposition 3; **read** by b). Own arithmetic with numbers from tracked files: the count-only network reaches 59.3% on the dev split (docs/research/lit-campaign/worker-brief.md:23), so mu = 0.407 and at alpha 0.10 at least 34% must stay unmarked, at most 66% can be sure per question; the end-to-end golden v2 run answers 2013 of 2948 (68.3%) with the dev prior (docs/PIPELINE.md:58), giving at most about 76%. Per plan the numbers are worse: a plan is "all right" only if every question about it is (ten independent answers at 90% give 35% of plans fully right, own arithmetic).

**5. Base rates from the one public benchmark of the task.** AECV-Bench, 120 floor plans, exact-match count accuracy of ten VLMs: doors 0.09-0.39, windows 0.10-0.34, bedrooms 0.73-0.91, toilets 0.67-0.82 for the five strongest rows (2601.04819, Table 1; **read**, PDF text re-checked). Report c quotes the abstract's "often 0.40-0.55" as the door/window figure; that is the mean over four fields, see Disagreements. A gate over such counters must refuse most answers, as in other fields (37.0% accepted at 5% unsafe, RetinaGuard 2611.10689; 52.1% at 98.5% precision, VERDICT).

Code: MAPIE BSD-3-Clause (GitHub API, pushed 2026-11-10; re-checked); LTT reference code MIT (re-checked); no weights. ## 4. Transfers from other fields

- **Document extraction with accept/review (invoices, receipts, forms).** Plays: one answer on one plan. Why: same contract, same error type; fused scores and an exact-binomial gate are published (rows 63). Breaks: our unit is the plan, answers correlate (design effect 1.84-2.45 on receipts), no template index. Code: VerifyDoc as is for the protocol only; the rest idea only.
- **Chemical-structure OCR with four readers (VERDICT).** Plays: counters. Why: agreement of distinct readers with a validity filter ranks right above wrong. Breaks: needs 3-4 counters that fail differently. Idea only.
- **Two OCR programs plus a dictionary (reCAPTCHA).** Plays: detector and parser; the dictionary is our plausibility rules. Breaks: no dictionary for integers. Idea only.
- **Pest and aphid counting confidence.** Plays: a symbol; quality and clutter features beside the detector's own count. Breaks: simulated traps, Jaccard target, small test set. Idea only (paper CC BY-NC-ND).
- **Histology follicle counting with LTT.** Plays: a window cell. Breaks: guarantee on precision, not exact count; 35 calibration cuts; no licence file. Idea only.
- **Driving: missed-object gates and per-condition thresholds (KGFP, SOTIF, weather CRC 2611.04305).** Plays: a missed window; conditions = scan/photo/rotation. Why: shows thresholds do not transfer between conditions, per-condition calibration works but false alarms rose from 1.6 to 117 per image. Breaks: YOLOv8 AGPL. Idea only.
- **Medical triage and screening.** Plays: a plan is an exam, the surveyor the radiologist. Why: triage (machine decides easy ones) beats second-reader mode in a meta-analysis (recalls -11%, reads cut 44-70%). Breaks: huge calibration sets. Idea only.
- **Election risk-limiting audits and acceptance sampling.** Plays: a batch of released plans. Why: sample size set by the margin between machine error and the promise (BRAVO: 22 draws at a 20-point margin, 8,157 at 2 points); sequential and hypergeometric designs for a finite delivery (own arithmetic: 26-47 plans of 200). Breaks: ballots have exact ground truth. Idea only.
- **Clerical QC: double keying, Census 2000, lab autoverification.** Plays: second reading, random independent sample, rule-based release. Why: measured floors (0.14% double-key error; 77-85% released, reviewers' kappa 0.39-0.63). Breaks: fields not drawings. Idea only.
- **Label aggregation (Dawid-Skene and successors).** Plays: several methods as noisy annotators. Breaks: shared inputs break conditional independence (2601.22336); for numbers the mean was best (Zheng et al., one dataset). Idea only.
- **N-version software and capture-recapture.** Plays: independent counters; two overlapping readings estimate misses. Breaks: independence; underestimates below four readers. Idea only.
- **Low-prevalence visual search (Wolfe 2005 and follow-ups).** Plays: the surveyor auditing a clean stream. Why: rare targets are missed (30-40% miss at 1-2% prevalence, 2010 study); seed known-wrong plans. Breaks: lab letters. Idea only.
- **Medical segmentation review (RACE-Triage).** Plays: marks a surveyor must check. Why: per-region risk control cut missed errors 31.8% to 19.3%; 100 local images recalibrated a new site. Breaks: per-pixel errors. Idea only.
- **LLM and VLM confidence.** Plays: a VLM counter. Why: verbalized and token confidence fail on counts; hidden-state probes and agreement across distinct judges do better. Breaks: open weights needed, counts 1-9, synthetic. Fine-tuning only.

## 5. Disagreements and which holds

1. **Door and window exact-match on AECV-Bench.** Report a: doors 9-39%; report c: "0.40-0.55". I opened the PDF text: Table 1 gives door/window 0.39/0.34 (Gemini 3 Pro), 0.28/0.27, 0.16/0.16, 0.09/0.17, 0.09/0.10; the abstract's "often 0.40-0.55" matches the mean over four fields (0.51, 0.49, 0.42, 0.37, 0.39), which includes easy bedrooms and toilets. Report a holds.
2. **Minimum calibration size.** a: 22 at zero errors, 44 with 10 thresholds, 47/65/80 for one to three errors; b: 22, 66 with 100 thresholds, 38-295 for error rates; c: 29 (delta 0.05), 73 (delta' 5e-4). I recomputed: exact tail, delta 0.10 gives 22/38/52/65; 100 thresholds 66; delta 0.05 zero errors 29. They differ in delta, thresholds tried and whether the factor e of the Hoeffding-Bentkus form is included (a includes it; b reads MAPIE's binary variant as dropping it, which I did not re-open). All hold for their stated settings; plan with the exact tail.
3. **How much data certifies.** b: 300 answers certify with AUC 0.98, 1,000 with 0.92; c: 134 units certify 18.5%, 800 receipts certify about 6% at document level. Not a conflict: b is synthetic with independent answers and strong scores, c is measured with plans as units and a gate near the target; ours resembles c.
4. **Count guarantee in SeqCRC.** a's first pass and b's early note said no count guarantee; later in a, b and c a one-sided count loss exists (box-count-threshold and recall) and an exact-equality loss is not monotone, so not controllable. The later reading holds; cods has a README MIT badge and no LICENSE file (GitHub licence field empty, re-checked): treat as unlicensed.
5. **Agreement and view spread.** a: count spread across augmentations near chance (KITTI); c: multi-view agreement helps (GRC, strings) and distinct-reader agreement helps (VERDICT); a, c: same-model sample agreement weak. Consistent once the readers are distinguished: agreement carries information when views or readers fail differently. Rotations of one detector are the untested middle case.
6. **Review cost per mark.** b: 1.6 s per yes/no check; c: 4.5 s per annotated instance. Both are in the sources: 1.6 s per check (re-checked, 1.6-2 checks per image), 4.5 s per instance is the 2017 paper's total; the extreme-clicking time is 7.2 s in a and b, 7.0 s in c (not re-opened; unresolved, immaterial).
7. **MAPIE default.** a and c note the default `bonferroni_holm`; only b reports it certifies nothing when a threshold accepts no points. Source default re-checked; the failure is unreplicated by me.
8. **ExtractConf base accuracy.** c states 74.2% and 73.3% in different places; the abstract says 26% failure (74% accuracy). Immaterial.

## 6. Negative results

- Mean or sum of detection confidences as an image score: top-100 mean correlates -0.58 to -0.62 with per-image AP; sum/mean AUROC 18.8-85.8 (2412.01782; 2307.00934).
- Count spread across augmentations alone: |rho| < 0.1 (2509.12871).
- Pixel re-render check of a prediction: AUROC 0.547 (2608.22183). Colour perturbation means little on black-and-white drawings (c).
- Token or verbalized confidence of a VLM for counts: above 90% when off by one (2605.08200); unchanged at 0.9 after 3x less visual evidence (2601.00138); AUROC 0.54-0.74 for extraction fields (2609.20110).
- Agreement among samples of one model: rho 0.20-0.59, confident errors recur, reasoning concentrates errors, majority vote hurt 56.6% and 65.7% of problems (2607.08065, 2609.32035, 2608.11403).
- Softmax and energy OOD scores cannot separate shifted in-domain from out-of-domain documents (AUC about 0.5, ZOD; 0.55-0.75, Larson).
- D-ECE and plain detector calibration ignore misses; NMS worsens calibration; per-detection post-hoc uncertainty AUROC 0.55 on COCO (GRACE).
- Conformal detection guarantees cover detected objects only; exact count equality is not monotone in a threshold, so no CRC for it; one-sided count control costs 11-16x boxes.
- Naive thresholds fit on calibration data break the budget (49-73%); a pooled gate fails at 40% of sites; thresholds do not transfer across conditions (2610.35092).
- Interval width as a selector: AUROC 0.72 for point error but 0.38 for interval misses (2611.06158, abstract).
- Capture-recapture below four readers underestimates (1997 report; simulation (b) 5-6x low).
- Learned scores (ConfidNet, DeepGamblers, text-to-SQL verifiers) do not generalise beyond their datasets; MSP unbeaten across failure sources (2211.15259); ensembles add 0.2-0.3 points over plain confidence on floor plans (Jakubik).
- Confidence flags and explanations do not reduce acceptance of wrong suggestions (2103.04725, 2006.14779); a reviewer shown half the boxes inherits the misses (2401.17821).

## 8. Experiments proposed

1. On dev, for every plan compute entry 5's P(modal count) and entropy, the top-k mean confidence, the rotation/tile count spread, two-counter agreement and the rule flags; label "answer right" and "all answers of the plan right"; report failure AUROC and AUGRC (2407.01032) per score by plan-level resplits. 2. Settles whether we can say the sentence at all: number of accepted plans at or above 22-65 and violation rate at or below 10%.
3. Count the dev plans where both of two different counters are wrong, one is wrong, none. Settles entry 3: if the both-wrong share exceeds half of the single-wrong share, agreement is a weak gate.

5. Timed review of about 20 plans with planted wrong marks, per-mark yes/no on magnified crops. Settles reviewer error and seconds per plan to put in the claim.
