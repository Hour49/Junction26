How we read a floor plan

Every file is classified first, now including its discipline. Plans are read and double-checked. Piping, heating and the other disciplines take the same route, models or pictures, into a fact sheet per discipline. Everything else becomes searchable evidence. Gemini 4 Carbon does the jobs our local models cannot.

Piping, heating and other disciplines

The organiser's hardest questions ask about pipes, heating, slabs and baseboards, and those come as building models or as pictures of drawings. The classifier now sends them down their own branch, the same way it sends floor plans: a model or vector file is parsed exactly, a picture is read with vision and double-checked, and both end in a fact sheet per discipline. An answer takes its numbers only from that sheet. Before tonight the pipeline answered none of the 649 practice questions on building models.
