# Vellum promo: "I'm missing a sauna"

Status: draft v1, 2026-11-14. Bogdan approves before filming. Facts and sources: [RESEARCH.md](RESEARCH.md).

Decisions (Bogdan, 2026-11-14): the product is called Vellum. The app screen starts as a mockup. The
structure is a funny hook first, then a serious part on how the app works and its key values.

## The idea in one line

He wants a sauna and a new door, and Vellum answers from the drawings. When it can't prove something, it
says so. Then we stop joking and show the real app doing the same thing on real drawings.

## Format

- 9:16 vertical master at 1080x1920. A 16:9 version for the deck comes from the same edit, with the phone
  footage pillarboxed.
- About 2:00 in total: act 1 is the skit (0:00-0:50), act 2 is the real app (0:50-1:50), and the close
  runs from 1:50 to 2:00.
- It has to work on mute, so every spoken line is also a burned-in caption and the music only sets the mood.
- Labels on screen: the act 1 corner reads `Dramatization`, the AI shots carry a small `AI-generated` tag,
  and the act 2 corner reads `Real app · built this weekend`.
- Vellum's look: a sheet of translucent drafting paper (vellum) slides over the real wall or the drawing,
  and plan lines show through. Red means no, green means yes, and amber means "can't tell". The vellum
  overlay is built in HyperFrames rather than generated, so every Vellum answer looks the same.
- Every Vellum answer card shows four things: the answer, the proof (the file, the page and a box on the
  drawing), a confidence, and what is still missing.

## What to film (real footage, phone camera, not WhatsApp)

- Use the apartment you already used, with the same t-shirt in every shot so the AI shots match.
- Shoot at 4K or 1080p, vertical, with the phone on a stand or held very still. Hold each shot 2 s longer
  than you need.
- The real shots are 1, 2, 4, 6, 8, 9, 10, 11 and 13, plus the bathroom wall and the blank wall under the
  TV as empty plates (5 s each, no person).
- AI start frames: end shot 6 on him facing the bathroom wall, and end shot 11 on him facing the TV wall
  with a broom held like a hammer.
- Props: a felt sauna hat if one is around, a binder with a printed `RAKENNESUUNNITELMAT` label, and a sofa
  cushion or a teammate with something wide for shot 13.
- Teammates: one person for shot 13 and one calm voice for act 2. Bogdan, or whoever reads best.
